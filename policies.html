Orders & payment

All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (RM). Online payments are processed on our payment provider's secure page. We never see or store your card details or online banking login.

  • Your order is confirmed once the payment goes through and the crew confirms it with you on WhatsApp.
  • If an item turns out to be out of stock after you've paid, we'll offer a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
  • Pre-order items show an estimated arrival time when you order. If that changes, we'll tell you and you can cancel for a full refund.
  • Fitting, mapping and tuning are quoted separately unless the product says otherwise.

Dyno booking deposits

A deposit holds your dyno slot. The amount is shown when you book, and it's deducted from your final bill.

  • Your slot is held once the crew confirms the booking on WhatsApp.
  • You can reschedule for free if you tell us at least 24 hours before your slot.
  • If you cancel at least 24 hours before your slot, the deposit is refunded in full.
  • If you cancel late or don't turn up, the deposit may be kept to cover the reserved dyno time.
  • If we have to move your slot, you can pick a new one or get the deposit back in full.

Pickup & delivery

Self-pickup is available at the shop during opening hours. Bring your order reference or payment receipt.

Delivery within Malaysia can be arranged on WhatsApp. We'll quote the courier charge before dispatch and send you the tracking number once the parcel is on its way.

Returns & refunds

  • Report a defective, damaged or wrong item within 7 days of receiving it. Send photos on WhatsApp and keep the original packaging.
  • Items must be unused and not installed, unless they are faulty.
  • We can't accept change-of-mind returns on electrical parts or ECUs once they've been opened or installed, or on items ordered specially for you.
  • Labour, mapping and dyno time already carried out are not refundable.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 14 business days.

Privacy

We handle your personal data under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

  • What we collect: your name, phone number, delivery address, bike details and order history.
  • Why we collect it: to process orders and bookings, arrange pickup or delivery, and contact you about your bike.
  • Who we share it with: only our payment provider and courier, and only as needed to complete your order. We never sell your data.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting us on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • This website: it uses no tracking cookies. Your browser stores your last booking reference so the "send on WhatsApp" prompt works when you come back from the payment page.
